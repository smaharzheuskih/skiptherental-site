🇮🇹 Car sharing in Italy

Easy in Milan, Rome and Turin; thin elsewhere

Free2Move covers Milan, Rome, Turin and five airports, with street parking included. Corrente runs electric cars around Bologna. Enjoy now starts and ends only at fixed Enjoy Points.

In November, 3 days and 300 km from Milan: car sharing €239 (Free2Move) against a typical rental at €194, so the rental is €45 cheaper.

Which one when

Milan, Rome or TurinFree2Move: pick up anywhere, park on public streets for free, end in the city you started.
Bologna, Ferrara or ParmaCorrente: electric cars that may enter Bologna's ZTL; €2–5 extra to end in another of its towns.
FlorenceEnjoy is the only service. Start and end at an Enjoy Point, and it can't enter the ZTL.
Straight from the planeFree2Move at Linate, Malpensa, Fiumicino, Ciampino and Turin; Corrente at Bologna.
A day out of townCorrente (150 km in a day) or Enjoy (50 km per package, fuel included). Free2Move charges every km on its day rate, and fuel is not included.

Airports

Milan Linate (LIN)

Free2Move bays in car park P2, level 6. The barrier opens by itself. €5.99 to start, €6.99 to end there.

Milan Malpensa (MXP)

Free2Move at Terminal 1: follow the car-sharing signs. €24.99 to start, €29.99 to end there.

Rome Fiumicino (FCO)

Free2Move and Enjoy on the roof terrace of the Terminal 1 short-stay car park, entered from car park B; plates are read at the barrier. Free2Move: €14.99 each way.

Rome Ciampino (CIA)

Free2Move: 12 bays in car park P5, €9.99 each way.

Turin (TRN)

Free2Move in the 'P Online Low Cost' car park, €11.99 each way.

Bologna (BLQ)

Corrente in the Waitzone, €5 extra to start or end there. You can book a car up to 24 hours ahead.

Between cities and countries

  • Free2Move: leave the city as long as you like, but bring the car back to the same Home Area to end, no Milan-to-Rome one-way.
  • Enjoy: drive anywhere in Italy; return to the starting Enjoy Point, or to another one if the start is a 'One Way' point.
  • Corrente: end in Bologna, Casalecchio, Imola, Ferrara, Parma or Reggio Emilia AV station; €2–5 extra between towns.

Parking and fuel

  • Free2Move: public street parking in the Home Area is included; private garages are at your cost.
  • Corrente: free on blue lines in Bologna, Ferrara and Casalecchio, and in Bologna and Casalecchio also in residents' bays. Never end in an underground garage.
  • Enjoy: since January 2026 you pay for blue lines and car parks yourself, outside the Enjoy Points.
  • Rome Termini station: 11 Free2Move bays, enter by car from Via Marsala 53.

Before you go

  • Non-EU licence: carry an International Driving Permit or an official Italian translation. The fine is €408–1,634.
  • Free2Move and ZTLs: Milan's Area C yes, its ZTLs no; Rome every ZTL except Tridente (A1) and Appia Antica; Turin not Area Romana or Valentino.
  • Free2Move: 18+, licence held 1 year; non-EU licences also need proof of entry into Italy. Enjoy: 18+, licence from any country held 1 year, credit or debit card (no prepaid). Corrente: 18+.
  • Corrente asks for a tax code at sign-up; if you live abroad, your home country's equivalent is accepted. It can take up to 10 days to approve a foreign licence, so register before you travel.

Be ready for

  • Malpensa is expensive: €24.99 to start and €29.99 to end a Free2Move trip.
  • Florence has only Enjoy, which no longer enters the ZTL or parks free on blue lines.
  • One-way between big cities is not possible with Free2Move.
  • Cars stay in Italy: Enjoy charges €250 for driving abroad, and so does Free2Move without its paid option — also for trips into Campania, Puglia, Calabria, Basilicata and Sicily.

Questions

Can I use car sharing in Italy with a licence from outside the EU?

All services open to visitors take licences from outside the EU in some form (Free2Move, Enjoy, E+Share Drivalia and Corrente). Each app checks your licence at sign-up; the table above says what each one accepts.

Do I need a credit card or a deposit?

Free2Move, Enjoy and Corrente may hold an amount on your card or ask for a deposit; the cards above say what the terms publish. E+Share Drivalia doesn't publish a deposit rule; the app shows it at sign-up. You add a bank card in the app and pay per trip. A rental desk usually wants a credit card in the driver's name and freezes a deposit.

Can I take a shared car out of Italy?

Free2Move: the trip has to end in Italy. Enjoy: Italy only. E+Share Drivalia: not published, ask before you cross. Corrente: Italy only.

Is car sharing cheaper than a rental car in Milan?

It depends on the trip. In November, one day and 60 km cost car sharing €72 (Free2Move) against a typical rental at €65, so the rental is €7 cheaper. Three days and 300 km cost car sharing €239 (Free2Move) against a typical rental at €194, so the rental is €45 cheaper. Check your own trip.

Can I pick up a car at the airport?

Milan Linate (LIN): Free2Move bays in car park P2, level 6. The barrier opens by itself. €5.99 to start, €6.99 to end there.
