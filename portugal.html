🇵🇹 Car sharing in Portugal

One service, by the day, Lisbon and Porto only

No car sharing by the minute in Portugal: Bolt Drive rents app-unlocked cars for 6 hours or more in Lisbon and Porto, back to the same spot.

Who's here

Bolt Drive

Automatic · small, mid-size, premium, SUVs and vans

Best for: A day out from Lisbon or Porto

  • Book 6 hours to 14 days, up to 30 days ahead; 250 km a day included.

Which one when

A day trip from Lisbon or PortoBolt Drive: at least 6 hours, back to the pick-up spot.
Arriving by planeBolt Drive lists the airport among its pick-up spots. Check the app before you fly.
Short hops in the cityNo car sharing by the minute, Bolt Drive starts at 6 hours.
One way, Lisbon to PortoNot with Bolt Drive: the car goes back where you took it; a rental car can do one way.

Airports

Lisbon (LIS) and Porto (OPO)

Bolt Drive says its pick-up spots include the airport; the exact car park and any fee are shown only in the app. Return the car to the same spot.

Between cities and countries

  • Bolt Drive is round-trip only: the car goes back to its pick-up spot in Lisbon or Porto.
  • No one-way trips between Lisbon and Porto. For that you need a rental car; rental firms add a one-way fee, shown when you book.

Parking and fuel

  • Parking is not included with Bolt Drive. You pay street parking and car parks yourself.
  • Return the car to the exact spot you picked it up from.

Before you go

  • Upload your licence in the Bolt app before you travel. Bolt shows which licences it accepts, and not all are.
  • Some motorways have no toll booths, only overhead gantries (electronic-only). Rental firms must fit an electronic toll device (Decree-Law 84-C/2022). Ask Bolt in the app how tolls are billed.
  • You rent from Bolt's partner rental firm: its terms and any deposit are shown in the app before you book.

Better than renting here

No rental desk: unlock with your phone
Automatic gearbox in every car at no extra cost
Basic insurance included in the price

Be ready for

  • Only one service, and only in Lisbon and Porto.
  • No per-minute trips and no one-way trips: 6 hours minimum, back to the same spot.
  • Fuel and parking are on you.
  • Bolt Drive does not publish how it bills tolls. Check the app.
