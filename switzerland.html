Which one when

Landing at ZurichMobility: 15 cars in car park P3, level 0Z, next to the airport train station.
A day in the mountainsMobility from the location nearest you, the vignette is already paid.
Staying in a villageLook at Sponti-Car's map: 60+ e-car locations, km included.
With a small childMobility: ISOFIX for two seats; bring your own seat.
Into France, Italy or GermanyNeither service allows it if you live outside Switzerland.

Airports

Zurich (ZRH)

Mobility has 15 cars at its 'Zürich Flughafen Bahnhof' station: car park P3, level 0Z, spaces 12–26, next to the airport train station.

Returning elsewhere

Zurich Airport says cars from this station can be returned to any Mobility station. Check this in the app when you book.

Between cities and countries

  • Mobility: 1,600+ locations; the car goes back where you took it, apart from the one-way Zurich Airport station.
  • Sponti-Car: 60+ e-car locations, many in smaller towns and in Romandie; always a round trip.

Parking and fuel

  • Each Mobility car has its own space at its location, included in the price.
  • Sponti-Car: bring the car back to its charging spot and plug it in. CHF 50 if you don't.

Before you go

  • Register online before you travel: Mobility takes people living abroad and needs an e-mail address and a credit card.
  • Sponti-Car checks your licence and a selfie within 48 hours. Register early.
  • Bringing a car from abroad? Swiss motorways need a vignette: CHF 40 at e-vignette.ch, valid 1 December to 31 January the year after.
  • Mobility cars have all-season tyres but no snow chains. Bring your own in heavy snow.

Be ready for

  • Round trips only. The car goes back where you took it.
  • If you live outside Switzerland, you may not take Mobility or Sponti-Car cars abroad.
  • Without the reduction, Mobility's excess is up to CHF 3,000. More for vans and young drivers.
  • Mobility charges a CHF 39.90 activation fee, even for one trip.
