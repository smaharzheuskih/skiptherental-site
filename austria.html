Which one when

A day in ViennaFree2move: free street parking in the Home Area; fuel included by the minute or hour, not on day rates.
Landing at Vienna airportFree2move: cars in car park P3, Level 0; €12.99 airport fee.
Graz, Linz, Salzburg or Innsbruck by trainÖBB Rail&Drive at the main station. Register a week ahead.
With a non-EU licenceFree2move (IDP or certified translation, plus proof of entry) or Rail&Drive (licences valid in Austria).
Into the Alps or a neighbouring countryRail&Drive: vignette included, Europe and Switzerland allowed. caruso goes to every neighbouring country.

Airports

Vienna (VIE)

Free2move only: car park P3, Level 0, next to the terminal. Take the lift to Level 2 for the covered walkway. The barrier opens by itself; airport fee €12.99.

Rail&Drive

No station at the airport. Its nearest Vienna cars are at city stations such as Hauptbahnhof, Westbahnhof and Praterstern.

Between cities and countries

  • Free2move: drive as far as you like, but end only in Vienna's Home Area or its drop-off zones.
  • Rail&Drive: 59 stations in all nine states. Eight in Vienna, plus Graz, Linz, Salzburg and Innsbruck main stations. The car goes back where you took it.
  • WienMobil Auto and caruso cars also go back to their own station.
  • Abroad: Rail&Drive allows Europe and Switzerland, caruso the neighbouring countries, WienMobil Auto only Germany; Free2move only with its paid option.

Parking and fuel

  • Free2move: street parking in Vienna's Home Area is free. Fuel is included by the minute or hour, not on day rates.
  • Rail&Drive: charging is in the time price; pay for fuel only if you bring the tank back below a quarter.
  • In car parks with a barrier, use the parking card or barrier key kept in the Rail&Drive car.
  • WienMobil Auto: return the car with at least 25% charge.

Before you go

  • Motorways need a vignette. Rail&Drive, caruso and tim cars have one; with the others, check before you drive. A digital 1-day vignette costs €9.60, 10 days €12.80 (2026).
  • Register early: Rail&Drive checks take up to 3–5 working days; caruso approves accounts only in office hours.
  • Licences: Free2move takes non-EU ones with an IDP or certified translation and proof of entry; Rail&Drive accepts international licences valid in Austria; caruso only EU, EEA and Swiss.
  • tim in Graz and Linz needs a membership with a 12-month minimum term, not made for a short visit.

Better than renting here

Free street parking in Vienna's Home Area
Free2move cars at Vienna airport
Rail&Drive at 59 stations in all nine states
Motorway vignette already in Rail&Drive and caruso cars

Be ready for

  • Only Vienna has free-floating cars; everywhere else the car goes back to its station.
  • Rail&Drive takes up to 3–5 working days to approve you, with a credit check.
  • Free2move, WienMobil Auto and GoMore don't say their cars carry the Austrian vignette. Check before the motorway.
  • Free2move charges €500 for a car left outside the Home Area.
