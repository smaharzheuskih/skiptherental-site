Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It's a hobby project. Nobody pays to be listed, there are no ads and no affiliate links, and you never sign up.

How do you count the rental price?

The typical price people book online for your city, dates and car, plus zero-excess insurance bought at the desk and fuel for your distance. Car sharing gets the same treatment: its fees are added, and so is its own cover that takes the excess down. Untick "Full cover" to compare both with their standard insurance instead. Everything we count.

Can I use car sharing with a non-EU licence?

Often yes. Choose "Other country" under More options and the answer flags anything that applies. Licence rules by service.

Is car sharing always cheaper?

No. Long drives and some cities favour a rental car, and the answer tells you when that happens. How often each one wins.
