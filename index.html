At home in Lithuania I switched to car sharing, and I made this free site so you can check it anywhere in Europe. No ads, no affiliate links.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It's a hobby project. Nobody pays to be listed, there are no ads and no affiliate links, and you never sign up.

How do you count the rental price?

The usual price people pay in your city and month, plus the zero-excess insurance sold at the desk, plus fuel for your distance. Car sharing gets the same treatment: its fees and damage cover are added too.

Can I use car sharing with a non-EU licence?

Often yes. Choose "Other country" under More options and the answer flags anything that applies.

Is car sharing always cheaper?

No. Long drives and some cities favour a rental car, and the answer tells you when that happens.
